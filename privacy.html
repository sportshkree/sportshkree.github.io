Privacy Policy – SportShkree Publisher

Last updated: September 29, 2026

SportShkree Publisher ("the app") is a private, single-user tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel SportShkree to upload the channel's own videos through the YouTube API Services. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly – to read the channel's ID, name and public statistics in order to confirm that uploads go to the correct channel.

The app does not access comments, subscribers' personal data, messages or any data of other YouTube users.

How data is used and stored

Data received from the YouTube API is used only to perform uploads and to verify the target channel. The OAuth access token is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never sent to any third-party server. The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties and does not use data for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the locally stored token is deleted by the owner and no further data is accessed.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not keep a copy of any YouTube API data. The only stored item is the OAuth token on the owner's computer. It is kept only while the app is in use and is deleted by removing the local token file or by revoking access on the Google security settings page above. Videos uploaded to the channel can be deleted at any time in YouTube Studio. To request deletion of any data related to the app, contact the address below; requests are handled within 7 days.
